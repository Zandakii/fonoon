Three generations of metalwork

Fonoon Engineering Industries grew out of the Alshishani company, founded in Jordan in 1969. We started in wrought iron construction and later opened an aluminium department focused on research and development.

Today we design, manufacture and install glass roofs and aluminium systems for homes, villas, restaurants and businesses from our factory in 6th of October.

  1. 1969Alshishani is founded in Jordan, building in wrought iron.
  2. ExpansionAn aluminium department opens, making doors and windows.
